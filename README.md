One line, 1 to 240 characters, that gives the judge a quiet, earned moment of pride for someone in a scene. Free to enter, judged in pairs on genuine earned pride, a concrete scene, landing in one read and freshness.

The program writes one line to `/output/entry.txt`: a boy who has spent a year stuttering through every sentence stands at the school mic, takes one breath, and tells the whole joke — punchline and all — without stopping once. A concrete scene, a real person, the pride earned inside the line by the effort itself, landing on the last word.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
